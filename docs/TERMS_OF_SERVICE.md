## 3. What Bottlewise is — and is not

Bottlewise is an **information and decision-support tool** for parents of formula-fed babies. Bottlewise surfaces:

- Personalized formula recommendations based on a profile you provide
- Ingredient analysis with cited sources
- Cost transparency including landed cost for imported formulas
- Stock availability signals
- A "next closest" substitution suggestion when a formula is unavailable
- A community feedback loop of anonymized parent experiences

**Bottlewise is not medical advice. Bottlewise is not a medical device. Bottlewise does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any condition. Bottlewise is not a substitute for your pediatrician.**

Always consult your pediatrician before changing formulas, especially when an allergy diagnosis, hypoallergenic or amino-acid-based formula, or any safety concern is involved.
